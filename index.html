PhD researcher · AI safety

Finding where machine learning models fall short

I am a PhD researcher at the Institute for AI Safety and Security of the German Aerospace Center (DLR) in Sankt Augustin. My research asks where a trained model is weak, rather than how good it is on average.

Model errors are not spread evenly across the input space but concentrate in compact regions, which I call weakspots. I develop statistical methods to locate them, and use them in two ways: to direct new training data to where a model is weak, and to direct tests to where it may be unsafe.

Research focus

  • Weakspot identification in regression models
  • Data-centric training guided by model weakness
  • Statistical testing and safety assessment of ML models
  • Uncertainty quantification and error landscapes

Research Environment

My experiments run in one shared environment: an interactive Streamlit application for configuring, running and inspecting experiments, backed by Python engines that run large sweeps from the terminal.
